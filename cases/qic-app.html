QIC App

Superapp connecting all insurance products and daily services on a single platform.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Company
QIC Digital Hub, Doha
Timeline
2024 – 2026
Platforms
iOS, Android, Web

01 Overview

QIC App is a unified insurance platform for managing car and travel insurance in Qatar. It brings policy purchase, claims, and lifestyle services together in one secure mobile experience, making it easier for customers to manage their insurance needs in one place.

Ecosystem for every need: general, motor, health and travel services around one app.

02 My role

I designed Marketplace experiences for Qatar’s first super app, helping scale QIC Market through customer-centered design, experimentation, and close collaboration with cross-functional teams.

03 What I designed

3.1

Main Page Integration

Integrated car market listings and a car status widget to increase engagement, conversion, and daily app usage.

3.2

Car Marketplace

Built a full car marketplace with intuitive filtering, listing cards, and a unique price calculator that evaluates each car against current market prices.

3.3

Listing Creation & AI Moderation

Designed the end-to-end listing creation flow — set the price, upload photos, complete vehicle details, and publish on QIC Market — with AI-powered photo moderation to ensure listing quality.

3.4

Car Preparation Services

Integrated car preparation services — detailing, washing, and repairs — with in-app payment and end-to-end service status tracking.

3.5

Paid Listing Promotion

Introduced paid listing promotion to increase visibility and help sellers sell their cars faster.
